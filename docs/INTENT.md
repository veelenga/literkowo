# Intent

## Why Literkowo exists

Literkowo teaches our children, aged 9 and up, to touch type in Polish and English.
It is a private family project, not a product.
It only works if it stays fun enough that the kids come back to it on their own.

## Who it is for

- Kids from 9 years old who are learning to type with all ten fingers.
- One shared family computer with a physical keyboard and several player profiles.
- Interface in Polish or Ukrainian, practice words in Polish or English.

## The one rule

Accuracy is the thing we reward.
Stars come from accuracy only, and a mistake always costs the combo.
Every new mechanic must keep careful typing as the best way to win.
If an idea makes the game more exciting but rewards speed over accuracy, it does not go in.

## Non-goals

- No accounts and no sign in.
- No server. The game is a static site on GitHub Pages.
- No ads.
- No data leaves the browser. Progress lives in localStorage on the device.
